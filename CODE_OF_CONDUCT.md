Code of Conduct

Our Standard

Sssssssddddd is committed to maintaining a professional, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who contributes to or interacts with our projects.

Participants are expected to:

* Be respectful and professional.
* Communicate clearly and constructively.
* Assume good faith while remaining open to evidence and correction.
* Focus criticism on ideas, implementations, and technical decisions rather than individuals.
* Accept constructive feedback.
* Respect different backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives.
* Protect private information and confidential material.
* Follow applicable project and platform rules.

Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

* Harassment or personal attacks.
* Discrimination or hateful conduct.
* Threats or intimidation.
* Deliberate disclosure of private or confidential information.
* Malicious disruption of project infrastructure.
* Trolling or sustained bad-faith behavior.
* Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual content.
* Impersonation intended to deceive or harm others.
* Publishing credentials, secrets, or sensitive data.
* Other conduct that materially prevents a safe and constructive technical environment.

Technical Disagreement

Strong technical disagreement is allowed and encouraged when conducted professionally. Arguments should be based on evidence, requirements, testing, documented behavior, or clearly identified assumptions. Disagreement is not a justification for personal attacks.

Enforcement

Project maintainers may take appropriate action in response to violations, including requesting changes to behavior, removing inappropriate content, restricting discussions, temporarily limiting participation, or removing access to project resources where necessary.

Serious or repeated violations may result in permanent loss of participation privileges.

Reporting

If you experience or observe behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, contact the maintainers privately. Do not use public issues or discussions to expose private information about another participant.

Security vulnerabilities must be reported according to SECURITY.md.

Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions within Sssssssddddd repositories, discussions, issues, pull requests, and other project spaces where it is adopted.
